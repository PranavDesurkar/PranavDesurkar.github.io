## Barger Leadership Institute | ML Research Lead - Skin of Color Diagnostics
**Duration:** 08/26/2024 - 05/12/2025 | **Location:** Ann Arbor, Michigan

<details markdown="1">
<summary><strong>Click to expand details</strong></summary>

![Research Poster](/assets/files/STF_Poster.pdf)

**Context:**  
This research project addresses the problematic discrepancy in dermatological diagnosis for Skin of Color (SoC) patients. Current ML-powered dermatology tools often replicate existing healthcare disparities, with 47% of dermatologists reporting inadequate training to diagnose conditions on skin of color. Our project focused on developing an equitable machine learning model for classifying skin conditions frequently misdiagnosed on SoC, starting with eczema.

**My Role:**  
I developed a comprehensive project proposal for an ML-powered diagnostic tool that addresses social biases in dermatological diagnosis, securing a $7,500 grant through demonstrated potential to deliver measurable health outcomes. I conducted stakeholder research with dermatologists, medical students, nursing students, and AI experts to gain insight on disparities in SoC healthcare education and sentiments surrounding AI in healthcare. I assembled a representative dataset consisting of 50% Skin of Color images (Fitzpatrick scale F3-F7) for both eczema and similar conditions. I trained a ResNet model on this augmented dataset and evaluated its performance against a standard dermatological classification model across all Fitzpatrick skin types (F1-F7).

**Impact:**
- Secured $7,500 in grant funding for research addressing healthcare disparities in dermatology
- Our SoC-catered model achieved 0.83 overall accuracy compared to 0.71 for standard models
- Achieved significantly improved performance on SoC skin types: 0.85 accuracy (vs 0.67 standard), 0.92 AUROC score (vs 0.50 standard), and 0.77 F1 score (vs 0.04 standard)
- Demonstrated that representative dataset augmentation led to more equitable classification performance across all skin tones

**Technical Stack:** Python, ResNet, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Statistical Modeling

**Work Samples:**
- [Research Poster](/assets/files/STF_Poster.pdf) - Full project presentation
- Final model and methodology documentation - Available upon request

**Key Skills:** #machine-learning #healthcare-equity #bias-mitigation #computer-vision #stakeholder-research
